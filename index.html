How to Prepare Your Device

Please follow these steps before you ship. We cannot buy devices that are locked to a personal account.

1. Back Up Your Data

Save your photos, files, and contacts to the cloud or an external drive. We will wipe all data permanently.

2. Sign Out of All Accounts

This is the most important step! Sign out of your Microsoft account on Windows, or turn off Find My and sign out of iCloud on a Mac. On phones and tablets, sign out of your Apple ID, Google, or Samsung account. Step-by-step guide

3. Factory Reset

After signing out, go to your settings and perform a full factory reset to erase your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you determine your offer?

Our offers are based on the device model, its cosmetic and functional condition, and current market value. We're a team of tech experts, and we aim to give the most competitive, fair price possible.

What happens if my device isn't as described?

This is our "Gotcha" policy: there is no gotcha. If we receive your device and its condition doesn't match your description, we'll email you a revised, fair offer. If you decline, we ship your device right back to you, 100% free of charge.

Is my personal data safe?

Absolutely. We professionally wipe all data from every device we receive as a standard part of our process. We'll also guide you on how to back up and wipe your device before you even ship it.

How long does it take to get paid?

Once we receive your device, we inspect it within 1-2 business days. If it matches your description, we pay you the same day by PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle.

How long is my offer good for?

14 days. Ship it within that window and, as long as the condition matches, that's what we pay.

What if it's lost or damaged in shipping?

Every label we send is prepaid and insured. If something happens in transit, that's on us, not you. Follow our packing guide to keep it safe.

Do I need to include the charger?

Please do. Offers assume the original charger is included. If you don't have it, just tell us and we'll adjust the offer up front.

Do you buy damaged or broken devices?

Yes. Cracked screens, weak batteries, even laptops that won't turn on. Pick "Has issues" or "For parts" on the form and tell us what's wrong.

Is there anything you can't buy?

We can't buy devices that are still locked to someone's account (iCloud, Microsoft, Google), have a BIOS password we can't remove, or are reported lost or stolen.
